Executive Summary

Nassau Candy Distributor — Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes the Nassau Candy Distributor dataset to understand sales performance, product profitability, and gross profit margins. An interactive dashboard developed using Python and Streamlit allows users to explore business performance across products and divisions.

Business Problem

Sales revenue alone does not provide a complete picture of business performance. Products with high revenue may not necessarily generate the highest gross profit. Businesses therefore need a way to compare product-level profitability, identify products requiring margin review, and understand how gross profit is distributed across the product portfolio.

Objectives

- Evaluate sales and gross profit across product lines.
- Compare gross profit margins across products and divisions.
- Examine the relationship between estimated cost and margin.
- Identify products contributing significantly to gross profit.
- Present the findings through an interactive dashboard.
- Recommend actions that could improve profitability and support business decisions.

Approach

The project uses Python and Pandas to prepare and analyze the dataset. Product-level and division-level aggregations are used to calculate sales, gross profit, and gross profit margins. Cost-versus-margin diagnostics highlight products for further investigation, while Pareto analysis examines the concentration of gross profit among products.

Streamlit brings these analyses together in an interactive dashboard with filters, visualizations, key performance indicators, and business recommendations.

Key Deliverables

1. Prepared dataset for analysis.
2. Product profitability and margin analysis.
3. Division performance comparison.
4. Cost-versus-margin diagnostic analysis.
5. Profit concentration and Pareto analysis.
6. Interactive Streamlit dashboard.
7. Research paper documenting the project methodology, findings, limitations, and recommendations.

Business Value

Business Value and Key Results

The dashboard provides an overview of the business's sales and gross profit performance using the selected dataset.

The reported key performance indicators are:

- Total Sales: $57,064.92
- Total Gross Profit: $37,724.48
- Total Orders: 3,462
- Total Units Sold: 15,575
- Gross Profit Margin: 66.11%

The reported gross profit margin indicates that gross profit represents approximately 66.11% of sales under the dashboard's calculation.

The product profitability analysis helps users compare products based on sales, gross profit, and gross profit margin. Division comparisons provide a way to examine differences in performance, while Pareto analysis helps assess the concentration of gross profit across products.

These insights can support further investigation into pricing, discounts, product-related costs, and product portfolio performance. The dashboard provides analytical support for business decisions, but additional investigation is needed to establish the causes of profitability differences.

Note: These figures reflect the dashboard values supplied for the current selection. Gross profit is not the same as net profit.

Recommendations

- Investigate products with low or negative gross profit.
- Review pricing, discounts, and product-related costs where appropriate.
- Monitor products that contribute strongly to gross profit.
- Compare division performance to identify areas for further investigation.
- Review profit concentration to understand reliance on a small number of products.
- Repeat the analysis periodically to monitor changes in performance.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis and interactive visualization can support product profitability and margin assessment. By combining product-level analysis, division comparisons, cost-versus-margin diagnostics, and Pareto analysis, the dashboard provides a practical foundation for data-driven business decisions.
